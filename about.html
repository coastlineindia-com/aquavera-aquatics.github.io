About AQUAVERA

AQUAVERA brings aquarium tanks, filtration, lighting, plants, hardscape, water care and maintenance into one focused experience. The goal is to make aquarium shopping easier to understand without turning the hobby into a generic pet-store catalog.

The website structure is inspired by the supplied reference site: clear task-focused navigation, dedicated information pages, strong calls to action and a footer that keeps support and policy resources easy to find.
