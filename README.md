# AQUAVERA Aquatic Co.

> **Life Flows Better.**

AQUAVERA is a premium aquarium and aquatic-supplies storefront created for aquarium keepers, aquascaping enthusiasts, freshwater hobbyists, planted-tank enthusiasts, and anyone building a healthy aquatic environment at home.

The brand brings together aquarium hardware, filtration, lighting, aquascaping materials, aquatic plants, water-care products, fish food, maintenance tools, and everyday aquarium essentials in one specialized shopping experience.

---

## 🌊 About AQUAVERA

AQUAVERA Aquatic Co. is built around a simple idea:

**A beautiful aquarium should also be a healthy aquarium.**

The store focuses on equipment and supplies that support the complete aquarium setup, from the tank and filtration system to lighting, substrate, plants, water care, feeding, cleaning, and aquascaping.

Rather than presenting a generic pet store catalog, AQUAVERA is organized specifically around aquarium ownership and aquatic environments.

## 🧭 Aquarium Setup Journey

AQUAVERA can guide customers through the main stages of building and maintaining an aquarium.

### 01 — Choose Your Tank

Select the aquarium size and format that fits the available space and intended setup.

### 02 — Build Filtration

Choose filtration based on tank size, livestock, and maintenance requirements.

### 03 — Add Lighting

Select appropriate lighting for the aquarium's visual appearance and, when applicable, aquatic plants.

### 04 — Establish Temperature

Choose heating and monitoring equipment appropriate for the aquarium environment.

### 05 — Create the Aquascape

Combine substrate, rocks, driftwood, and aquatic plants to create the desired layout.

### 06 — Maintain Water Quality

Use water conditioners and testing equipment as part of a consistent maintenance routine.

### 07 — Feed & Maintain

Choose appropriate food and keep the aquarium clean with dedicated maintenance tools.

## 📸 Photography Direction

Product photography is an important part of the AQUAVERA experience.

### Product images should:

- Show the complete product
- Be correctly framed
- Use clean backgrounds where appropriate
- Keep the product centered
- Avoid unnecessary cropping
- Avoid screenshots
- Avoid prices embedded into photographs
- Avoid buttons and interface elements inside photographs
- Avoid unrelated products entering the frame
- Maintain consistent lighting and image quality

### Lifestyle imagery can include:

- Planted aquariums
- Aquascapes
- Aquarium cabinets
- Close-up aquatic plants
- Filtration setups
- Clean home aquarium environments

Product cards should prioritize clear product presentation, while lifestyle photography should be reserved for hero, editorial, and collection sections.

## 🔐 Privacy & Data

The frontend can operate without a backend database for the core demonstration experience.

Cart and wishlist information may be stored locally in the user's browser using `localStorage`.

Sensitive payment information should never be stored directly in the frontend.

For production deployment, customer accounts, payments, orders, and personal information should be handled through secure backend infrastructure and appropriate payment providers.
